Storyboard v1

How a user moves through the website, one frame per section. Each frame shows what the user sees, what they do, what changes on screen, and the message they should take away. Group 5, COS30045.

  1. Frame 1: Title

    Sees
    Headline, one-sentence summary and a data note about counts and reporting changes.
    Does
    Reads, then scrolls down or uses the menu.
    Changes
    Page scrolls to Question 1.
    Message
    Over 39,000 people were hospitalised after road crashes in 2021; this story explains how that changed.

  2. Frame 2: National trend (Q1)

    Sees
    Line chart, 2011–2021, with notes at 2012 (Vic), 2017 (NSW) and 2020.
    Does
    Hovers over a year.
    Changes
    Point highlights; tooltip shows the year and count.
    Message
    Counts rose from 34,033 to 39,505, peaked in 2019 and dipped in 2020; reporting changes affect comparisons.

  3. Frame 3: Road users (Q2)

    Sees
    Diverging bars showing each road user's change from 2011 to 2021.
    Does
    Switches between "Change in count" and "Change in share"; hovers a bar.
    Changes
    Bars resize; tooltip shows the exact value.
    Message
    Pedal cyclists rose the most (+2,772); pedestrians fell (−420).

  4. Frame 4: States and territories (Q3)

    Sees
    Eight small line charts, one per state or territory.
    Does
    Hovers over one state's chart.
    Changes
    That chart highlights; tooltip shows its 2011 and 2021 counts.
    Message
    Queensland grew most (6,322 to 10,447); NSW fell, partly because of its 2017 counting change.

  5. Frame 5: Age and sex (Q4)

    Sees
    Population pyramid: males on the left, females on the right.
    Does
    Switches the year between 2011 and 2021.
    Changes
    Bars animate to the other year, so the shift in the age profile is visible.
    Message
    Males aged 40–64 were the largest group in 2021 (8,702); older age groups grew.

  6. Frame 6: First Nations Peoples (Q5)

    Sees
    Annual line chart, plus road-user shares compared with national shares.
    Does
    Hovers over the line and the bars.
    Changes
    Tooltips show counts and shares; "n.p." years appear as labelled gaps, not zeros.
    Message
    Counts rose from 1,150 to 2,214, peaking at 2,290 in 2020; motorcyclist hospitalisations rose from 187 to 520.

  7. Frame 7: Takeaways

    Sees
    Three key takeaways, a limitations box and links to the data sources.
    Does
    Reads, then clicks a source link if they want more detail.
    Changes
    Source opens in a new tab (BITRE or the Road Safety Data Hub).
    Message
    What the data shows, and what it can't show: counts are not population-adjusted and reporting changed over time.
Data: BITRE Hospitalised Injury (September 2023) and Road Safety Data Hub.
